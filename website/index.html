Built for Precision, Designed for Speed

Ditch outdated simulators and spreadsheets. NordSonic merges acoustic science with structural design into one unified fluid workspace.

Acoustic Simulation

High-accuracy physics modeling for Sealed, Vented (Bass Reflex), Passive Radiator, and 4th Order Bandpass (BP4) enclosures using Thiele/Small equations.

Auto-Solver & Cutting Lists

Input your target volume, and NordSonic immediately designs the cabinet panels. Supports double baffles, internal braces, and wedge-angled designs with bevel calculations.

Physical Fit Checks

Real-time warning notifications for physical driver magnet collisions, basket depth bounds, and acoustic port opening blockages (minimum 1x diameter spacing).

Advanced Port Engineering

Model straight slot and round ports, or design 90° bent ports. Includes precise velocity chuffing analysis and Flare-it compliant safety margins.

All-in-One Audio Toolkit

Includes Ohm's Law solvers, series/parallel speaker wiring impedance animators, wire sizing estimators (based on current/length), and db/SPL comparisons.

Built-in Tone Generator

Test your constructed cabinets directly from the application. Generate sine wave test sweeps to check for panel rattles, port noise, and air leaks.

During the Open Beta phase, all features are fully available to test. The application generates a secure 4-factor local hardware fingerprint (binding your CPU ID, CPU arch, hostname, and MAC address) to authorize a free trial period. You do not need to enter payment details to test the software.
