# NordSonic Open Beta v0.1.24 (Initial Release)

Welcome to the first public **Open Beta** release of **NordSonic**—a modern, high-precision loudspeaker enclosure design and acoustic simulation suite. 

We are excited to open testing to our serious speaker builders and DIY enthusiasts to help refine and polish the platform before the official launch.

---

## ✨ Features Included in the Beta Client

*   **Acoustic Modeling:** Simulate Sealed, Vented (Bass Reflex), Passive Radiator (PR), and 4th Order Bandpass (BP4) enclosures using precise Thiele/Small equations.
*   **Cabinet Dimensions & Cutting List Solver:** Auto-calculate panel cutting lists supporting double-front baffles, shelf window-cutout bracings, and angled wedge cabinets.
*   **Physical Fit & Interference Checks:** Real-time feedback warnings for driver magnet collisions, basket depths, and port spacing clearances.
*   **Advanced Port Flare Design:** Model round, straight slot, or 90° bent ports with velocity chuffing analysis and Flare-it compliant safety margins.
*   **Acoustic Toolkit:** Series/parallel wiring animators, Ohm's law calculators, db/SPL logarithmic converters, and wire-gauge estimators.
*   **Built-in Tone Generator:** Output test frequencies directly from the app to test cabinet rattle or port noise.

## 🔑 How to Activate the Open Beta

To prevent server overload and ensure high-quality testing feedback, we are limiting access to **1,000 testers**.

1.  **Download the Client:** Grab the executable for your platform (download the Linux client below, or Windows once posted).
2.  **Basic Mode:** By default, the application launches in **Free Basic Edition** (advanced calculators, geometry lists, passive radiator, and bandpass models will be locked).
3.  **Activate Beta:** Go to the top menu bar, select **File > Activate License...**, and enter one of our designated beta keys (format: `NS-BETA-XXXX-YYYY`).
4.  **Enjoy 60 Days Full Access:** Entering a valid beta key instantly unlocks all **Home** and **Pro** features for 60 days of testing.

> 💡 *To request one of the 1,000 active beta keys, please contact us directly or check our official release announcement channels!*
